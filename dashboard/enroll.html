Capture steps

The order is randomised each time — a photograph held up to the camera cannot follow it.

    Fatigue baseline

    25 seconds of alert, relaxed behaviour. This becomes this operator's personal "normal" for eye closure and blink rate — so when they sit down tomorrow, the machine already knows their baseline and does not have to re-measure it.

    Keep your eyes open and blink normally. A baseline recorded while already tired would make real fatigue look normal later — it will be rejected.
